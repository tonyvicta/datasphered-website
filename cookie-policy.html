Cookie Policy

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file made up of letters and numbers that is downloaded to your computer when you access certain websites. Typically, cookies allow a website to recognize a user device.

The cookies placed by this website are used to make it more user friendly, for example by storing site preferences and language settings.

2. Why do we use cookies?

We may use cookies and similar technologies for various purposes, such as:

  • For security and fraud prevention, and to detect and prevent cyber attacks
  • To provide selected services
  • To monitor and analyze the performance, operation, and effectiveness of our services
  • To enhance the user experience

3. Your options

To learn more about cookies, including how to see which cookies have been set and how to manage, delete, or block them, we recommend visiting aboutcookies.org or allaboutcookies.org.

Alternatively, you can configure your browser to block cookies altogether. To do this you need to change the cookie settings in your browser. These settings are usually found in the browser menu under Options or Preferences. Cookie settings are available in Firefox, Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, Safari on macOS and iOS, and Android.

Deleting our cookies or disabling future cookies or tracking technologies may result in certain areas or features of our services becoming unavailable or otherwise affecting your user experience.

To opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites, you can follow the instructions at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

4. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We encourage users to check this page regularly to stay informed about how we use cookies.
